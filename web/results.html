⚠️ Read before quoting numbers
  • One machine. RTX 3060, Ryzen 5 5600, Windows WDDM. The host-bound finding depends on WDDM's kernel-launch cost and will be weaker on Linux.
  • Batch size 1, greedy only. No sampling, no batching.
  • Regimes ran on slightly different prompt sets. 21 prompts were fixed between the eager and graphed sweeps; math, reasoning and prose are directly comparable, code and translation are not.
  • Eval set provenance. 12 prompts hand-written; 198 drafted with a 32B model and hand-edited, 21 of them substantively rewritten.
  • Graphed mismatches are not individually classified. Their rate matches eager, consistent with the same fp16-tie cause, but that is inferred.
  • Speculative decoding is not new. Leviathan et al. 2022; Chen et al. 2023. The contribution here is measurement on consumer hardware.
